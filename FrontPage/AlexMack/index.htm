Welcome to GC-161.com!

The number one fan page dedicated to Nickelodeon hit TV show "The Secret world of Alex Mack" starring Larissa Oleynik.

Alex Mack was walking home from school when she gets hit by a truck from the chemical plant. Doused in GC-161, a chemical with unknown properties, she developped powers such as telekinesis, the ability to morph in a puddle, and zap electricity out of her fingers. The chemical plant led by Danielle Atron has been looking for her ever since.

Alex has kept the accident from her parents, George and Barbara Mack, only revealing her secret to her sister and science genius Annie, and her best friend Ray. With their help, Alex has been able to thwart the plant's attempts at finding her.

Tune in on Saturdays at 8pm/7c, only on Nickelodeon!
